Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Purpose

Personal Finance Tracker is a private, non-commercial application developed exclusively for personal financial analysis.

Banking Data

The application accesses bank account information and transaction history through Enable Banking, subject to the account holder's explicit consent.

Data Storage

Banking data is stored locally on the user's computer. The application is designed not to synchronize banking data to third-party cloud storage services.

Data Sharing

Banking data is not sold or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Enable Banking and the connected bank process information as necessary to provide the banking connection.

Access and Deletion

The user controls the locally stored data and may delete it or revoke banking access through the relevant services.
