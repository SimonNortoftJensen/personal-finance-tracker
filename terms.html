Terms of Use

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Purpose

Personal Finance Tracker is a private, non-commercial application developed exclusively for personal financial analysis.

Personal Use

The application is intended solely for use by its developer with their own bank accounts. It is not offered as a public or commercial financial service.

Bank Account Access

The application uses Enable Banking to access account information and transaction history with the explicit consent of the account holder.

Data Handling

Retrieved banking data is processed locally on the user's computer. Data handling is described in the application's Privacy Policy.

Limitations

The application is provided for personal informational purposes. It does not provide financial, investment, or payment advice.

Availability

Access to banking data depends on the availability of Enable Banking, the connected bank, and the account holder's authorization.
